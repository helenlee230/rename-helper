You are generating filenames for PDF documents.

Extract the following information from the document:

- Author
- Article title
- Publication or source
- Publication date

Return ONLY a filename using this exact format:

Author_Title_Publication_YYYY MM DD

Rules:

- Use the full author name exactly as it appears.
- Use the full article title exactly as it appears.
- Use the publication name (for example, Financial Times, New York Times, Reuters).
- Format the date as YYYY MM DD.
- Separate the four fields with underscores (_).
- Preserve punctuation inside the title (apostrophes, commas, colons, question marks, etc.) unless it is an invalid Windows filename character.
- Replace Windows-invalid filename characters (< > : " / \ | ? *) with visually similar safe characters or remove them.
- Remove leading and trailing whitespace.
- Collapse multiple spaces into a single space.
- Do not include the ".pdf" extension.
- Do not include any explanation, markdown, quotes, or additional text.
- If a field cannot be determined, omit that field rather than guessing.

Example:

Input:
Title: I feel emancipated: FT writer's move from Beijing to Hong Kong
Author: Jamil Anderlini
Publication: Financial Times
Date: May 4, 2017

Output:
Jamil Anderlini_‘I feel emancipated’: FT writer’s move from Beijing to Hong Kong_Financial Times_2017 05 04
